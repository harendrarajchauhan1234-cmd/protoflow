Active Ingredients

Active ingredients are the primary components of a supplement that are intended to provide its nutritional or other functional properties. Their purpose depends on the specific formulation, concentration, and available scientific evidence.

Vitamins and Minerals

Vitamins and minerals are essential nutrients involved in normal body functions, including energy metabolism, cellular processes, and the maintenance of general health. If included in Protoflow™, their individual amounts should be checked on the product label.
🍁

Botanical Extracts

Botanical extracts are derived from plants and may contain naturally occurring compounds. Their properties vary according to the plant species, the part used, the extraction method, and the concentration of the extract.
🍁

Antioxidant Compounds

Some supplement ingredients provide antioxidant activity. Antioxidants help protect cells from oxidative damage under normal physiological conditions, although the effects of a particular supplement depend on its actual ingredients and formulation.
🍁

Metabolism-Related Nutrients

Certain nutrients participate in normal metabolic processes, helping the body use nutrients from food. However, the presence of a nutrient does not automatically mean that a supplement increases metabolism or produces measurable changes in body weight.
🍁

Supporting Nutritional Ingredients

Some formulas contain additional ingredients to complement their primary components. These may serve nutritional or formulation purposes, depending on the manufacturer's recipe.
🍁

Capsule Ingredients

In addition to active ingredients, capsules may contain excipients, such as materials used to form the capsule or maintain product stability. These ingredients should also be identified on the official packaging when applicable.
🍁

Ingredient Dosages and Quality

The amount of each ingredient matters when evaluating a supplement. Reviewing the ingredient quantities, serving size, quality information, allergen warnings, and recommended use can help consumers make informed decisions.
